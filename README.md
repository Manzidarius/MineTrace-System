# MineTrace-System

**MineTrace System** is a digital mine management and mineral traceability platform designed to monitor the movement of minerals from extraction to the final buyer. 
It enables mining companies, regulators, and traders to record production, track mineral shipments, manage inventory, and generate compliance reports.
By providing real-time tracking and transparent records, MineTrace helps prevent illegal mining, improves accountability, ensures regulatory compliance, and supports responsible mineral sourcing.
